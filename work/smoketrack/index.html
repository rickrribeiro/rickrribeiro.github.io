Overview

SmokeTrack is a small product with a personal purpose: help people (me first) notice the patterns behind their smoking. Everything stays on the device — no account, no backend.

The problem

Quitting apps either nag or demand accounts. I wanted something private that turns a simple log into insight.

What I did

React + TypeScript + Vite with Tailwind, local-first storage, Recharts for analysis and a PWA plus a Capacitor iOS build. Additive JSON import/export with validation and data migration keeps data portable as the model evolves.

What the analysis shows

  • Daily, weekday and weekend averages, and a daily chart with a 7-day moving average.
  • Intervals between records (mean, median, p90) and the longest gap without smoking.
  • An hour × weekday heatmap and the most frequent triggers (activity × time of day) compared with the average.
  • Breakdowns by activity, type and mood, plus a comparison with the previous period.
  • A 365-day consumption calendar and a per-day timeline that ignore the filters.
  • Filters by period or calendar month, weekdays or weekends, totals or daily averages.

Engineering challenges

  1. Making analysis useful with only a few data points a day.
  2. Evolving the data model safely on users' devices.

Outcomes

  • Shipped as a PWA on GitHub Pages and packaged for iOS with Capacitor.
  • Record editing, mood and notes added without breaking existing data, thanks to the migration layer.
  • Analytics covered by Vitest unit tests.

Lessons

  • Privacy is a feature: no account means no friction to start.
